REVISION FIRST, NEVER RUSHED

Keeping every surah strong alongside school life

Memorising quickly and forgetting a month later discourages both child and parent. At Tarteel House, memorisation lessons are built around small new portions paired with steady revision of older surahs, so your child actually keeps what they learn.

Every lesson is a live 40-minute one-to-one session on Zoom or Google Meet with a real teacher who hears every verse carefully. Your child learns new lines with clear pronunciation from the beginning rather than fixing habits later.

Your child normally continues with the same dedicated teacher, and every two months we invite parents to a 15-minute progress review. If a parent is unavailable or does not want a meeting, we send a visual progress report through WhatsApp instead.

A CALM ROUTINE

How a memorisation lesson is structured

Each 40-minute lesson balances revision, careful new listening, and a realistic plan for short home practice between classes.

1. Recent & older revision

Lessons open by reciting earlier surahs aloud so previous memorisation stays confident and ready for daily prayers.

2. Small new portions

New verses are repeated line by line with the teacher first, checking pronunciation and joining before moving on.

3. Clear home focus

Every session ends with a small, specific target for ten to fifteen minutes of calm practice at home, with no guesswork for parents.

4. Stages & certificates

A certificate is issued once a child has completed at least 30 lessons, finished an identifiable learning stage, and had completion confirmed by their teacher.

QUESTIONS PARENTS ASK

Before booking memorisation lessons

Can young children memorise before reading fluently?

Yes. Younger children (ages 5 to 7) often memorise short surahs by listening and repeating with their teacher while also learning Arabic letters through Noorani Qaida.

Which languages do teachers speak?

Lessons are taught in English, Arabic, Turkish, or Persian. Families are normally contacted within two days on WhatsApp after requesting a free trial.

What if my child resists home practice?

We keep goals small so memorisation stays warm rather than tense. Our guide on restarting gently when a child refuses practice shares practical steps.

Planning for Ramadan 2027?

Starting before February helps your child settle into a calm surah goal before the month begins. See our Ramadan 2027 classes & printable tracker.
