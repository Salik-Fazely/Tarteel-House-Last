START BEFORE THE MONTH BEGINS

Why families in Europe and North America begin a few weeks early

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 7–8 February, during the school term. Starting one-to-one lessons a few weeks earlier lets your child get comfortable with their dedicated female teacher and choose one realistic Ramadan goal before evenings get busy.

Packages are valid for one year from the purchase date, so the routine your child builds for Ramadan does not stop at Eid — it continues smoothly through the rest of the year with the same teacher.

Every lesson is 40 minutes long over Zoom or Google Meet. Every two months, we invite parents to a 15-minute progress review. If a parent is unavailable or does not want a meeting, we send a visual progress report through WhatsApp instead.

CHOOSE ONE CLEAR FOCUS

What your child can work toward this Ramadan

During the free trial, your child’s teacher listens to their level and helps you pick one calm, achievable goal.

Ages 5–7: Letters & first surahs

Gentle Noorani Qaida practice paired with one or two short surahs recited together after iftar.

Ages 8–11: Steady reading & Juz Amma

Fluent Mushaf reading, applied Tajweed, or two to four short surahs memorised and revised calmly.

Ages 12–16: Fluency & revision

Polishing recitation accuracy, securing earlier memorisation, and building a post-Ramadan habit.

Four teaching languages

Lessons in English, Arabic, Persian (Farsi & Dari), or Turkish, matched around your family’s school schedule.
