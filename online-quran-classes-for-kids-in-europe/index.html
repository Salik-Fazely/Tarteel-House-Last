BUILT AROUND EUROPEAN FAMILY LIFE

Calm one-to-one teaching without the school-night commute

Many Muslim families across Europe want a steady Qur’an teacher for their children, but local weekend schools can be crowded, far from home, or taught in a language their child does not yet speak comfortably. Tarteel House is based in Barcelona, Spain, and teaches families across European time zones (GMT, CET, and EET) as well as North America.

Every lesson is a live 40-minute one-to-one session with a real human teacher on Zoom or Google Meet — never a self-study app or pre-recorded course. Your child normally continues with the same dedicated teacher, matched personally by language, level, learning needs, and your family’s school-week availability.

Every two months, we invite parents to a 15-minute progress review. If a parent is unavailable or does not want a meeting, we send a visual progress report through WhatsApp instead.
