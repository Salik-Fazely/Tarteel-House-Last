WHY THE LANGUAGE MATTERS

The Qur’an in Arabic, the explanation in Persian

The Qur’an is always recited in Arabic. What changes is the language around it: how a teacher explains a sound, corrects gently, encourages, and speaks with you about your child.

Many children are more at ease when the teacher speaks the language they hear at home. At Tarteel House, lessons are held in Persian for Farsi-speaking and Dari-speaking families in Europe and the US.

Every lesson is one-to-one and lasts 40 minutes, and your child normally continues with the same dedicated teacher.

QUESTIONS PARENTS ASK

Before you book

Is the Qur’an taught in Persian?

The Qur’an is recited in Arabic. Your child’s teacher explains, corrects and encourages in Persian.

Do you teach Dari-speaking children?

Yes. Lessons are held in Persian for both Farsi-speaking and Dari-speaking families.

Can lessons include English?

Three of these teachers also teach in English, which can help children who move between both languages.

How do we book or ask a question?

The booking form is in English and takes a few minutes. Before booking, you can write to us on WhatsApp using the link at the bottom of this page.
