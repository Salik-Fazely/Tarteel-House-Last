SAFEGUARDING & PARENT TRUST

Designed for comfort, safety, and clear parent oversight

Many parents specifically look for a calm female teacher for their daughters and younger sons. At Tarteel House, your child normally continues with the same dedicated teacher throughout their package, so lessons feel warm and familiar rather than shifting from tutor to tutor.

All scheduling and service communication are managed through a parent or guardian. Private contact with a child requires prior parent or guardian permission and involvement. Lessons take place on Zoom or Google Meet, are not recorded by Tarteel House by default, and for younger children we ask a parent or guardian to stay nearby.

Every two months, we invite parents to a 15-minute progress review. If a parent is unavailable or does not want a meeting, we send a visual progress report through WhatsApp instead.
