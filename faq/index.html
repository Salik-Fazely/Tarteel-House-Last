EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

12 questions parents ask most

Tarteel House is a live one-to-one online Qur’an school taught by human female teachers over Zoom or Google Meet, not an automated app or pre-recorded video course.

We teach girls and boys aged 5 to 16, from complete beginners starting with Noorani Qaida to older students working on Tajweed and memorisation. We do not currently offer adult classes.

The Qur’an is always recited in Arabic. Explanations, encouragement, and parent communication are available in English, Arabic, Turkish, and Persian (for both Farsi-speaking and Dari-speaking families).

Both the free trial lesson and every paid one-to-one lesson last 40 minutes. There is no payment and no commitment required for the trial.

After your free trial, packages are Starter (6 lessons for €120, €20 per lesson), Steady (12 lessons for €220, approximately €18.33 per lesson), and Committed (25 lessons for €400, €16 per lesson). One package covers one child and is valid for one year from the purchase date. See full pricing details.

Tarteel House has a small team of four female Qur’an teachers. Parents may express a preference when booking, and we personally match each child based on language, level, learning needs, and scheduling availability. Your child normally continues with the same dedicated teacher.

Give at least 24 hours’ notice and the lesson is rescheduled without using a make-up allowance. Teacher or Tarteel House cancellations are always rescheduled outside the allowance. The 12- and 25-lesson packages include up to four make-up lessons for late-notice or unnotified absences, while the 6-lesson package has no allowance for absences without sufficient notice.

Every lesson ends with a clear focus for short practice at home. Every two months, we invite parents to a 15-minute progress review. If a parent is unavailable or does not want a meeting, we send a visual progress report through WhatsApp instead.

Scheduling and service communication are managed through a parent or guardian. Private contact with a child requires prior parent or guardian permission and involvement. Lessons run on Zoom or Google Meet and are not recorded by Tarteel House by default.

Tarteel House is based in Barcelona, Spain, and serves Muslim families across Europe (including Germany, the UK, the Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, France, Austria, Switzerland, Spain, and Ireland) and North America, scheduled around local school timetables.

A certificate is issued only when a child has completed at least 30 lessons, completed an identifiable learning stage, and had completion confirmed by their teacher. Purchasing a package alone does not qualify for a certificate.

Families are normally contacted on WhatsApp within two days to arrange the free trial and next steps. If you have a question before booking, WhatsApp is our preferred pre-booking route.
