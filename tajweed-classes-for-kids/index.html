WHY LIVE CORRECTION MATTERS

Learning Tajweed through listening and gentle practice, not dry rules

The word tarteel means reciting the Qur’an clearly and calmly, giving every letter its right. Children do not need heavy technical terminology to build strong Tajweed. What they need is an attentive human teacher who models the sound clearly, listens to every verse, and corrects gently in the moment.

In a group session, each student may only recite for a few minutes. In our one-to-one 40-minute lessons, your child reads aloud throughout the session while their dedicated teacher focuses on one clear point at a time.

Lessons are available in English, Arabic, Turkish, and Persian for families in Europe and the US. Every two months, we invite parents to a 15-minute progress review. If a parent is unavailable or does not want a meeting, we send a visual progress report through WhatsApp instead.

WHAT CHILDREN PRACTISE

Practical Tajweed matched to age and reading level

All four teachers on our Teachers page teach Tajweed. We introduce rules in a child-friendly order so recitation feels calm and steady.

Clear articulation (Makharij)

Distinguishing heavy and light letters and practising sounds that do not exist in English, German, Dutch, or French.

Applied Noon & Meem rules

Practising ghunnah, idgham, ikhfa, and izhar directly inside the surahs your child reads and recites in prayer.

Rhythm, lengthening & stops

Keeping madd counts balanced and learning where to pause and continue calmly without rushing through verses.

Confident independent reading

Moving from repeating after the teacher to spotting rules independently in the Mushaf, supported by short home reading between lessons.

QUESTIONS PARENTS ASK

Common Tajweed questions

Does my child need to read fluently first?

No. Children who already read short surahs can start Tajweed straight away; beginners start with Noorani Qaida and build good pronunciation from day one.

Can Tajweed be combined with memorisation?

Yes. Many families combine applied Tajweed with Juz Amma memorisation so children learn every surah with sound pronunciation from the start.

How do parents follow progress?

Each lesson ends with a clear home focus, and every two months we invite parents to a 15-minute progress review (or send a visual WhatsApp progress report). See how to track progress.

Are certificates available?

A certificate is issued once a child has completed at least 30 lessons, finished an identifiable learning stage, and had that stage confirmed by their teacher.
